    Inrico BT02 (B02) — Budget / Experimental Speaker-Mic¶

    Status: Experimental / Limited Support — Subject to Removal. The Inrico BT02 (also sold as the Inrico B02) is included as a budget option for testing because of community interest in supporting lower-cost Bluetooth speaker-mics. It is not a recommended replacement for AINA hardware, receives limited support, and may be removed in a future release if it proves unreliable in field traffic. For production use, choose the AINA PTT Voice Responder V2 (#1 recommended) or AINA PTT Voice Responder V1 (#2 recommended).

    Why it's included (and what "budget option" means)¶

    Operators frequently ask whether XVoice can work with inexpensive Zello-style Bluetooth speaker-mics. The Inrico BT02 meets the minimum criteria for basic testing (Classic HFP speaker/mic audio, physical PTT, and a dedicated SOS button), so it is supported experimentally as a budget tier.

    With a budget device come budget capabilities and hardware limitations:

    • Lower microphone gain and audio fidelity compared to AINA V1/V2 speakermics. XVoice applies a +5 dB (1.78x) software mic-gain boost automatically when capturing over BT02 Bluetooth SCO, but acoustic clarity and wind/noise rejection remain below AINA hardware.
    • Emergency (SOS) button ONLY triggers — it CANNOT disable/disarm an emergency: The BT02 firmware requires a 2-second press-and-hold before transmitting its emergency event (0x08) and never sends a release event. Because of this hardware limitation, holding SOS for 2 seconds only fires ATAK's emergency beacon — it does not cancel or turn it off. If you trigger an emergency with this budget speaker-mic, you MUST disable/disarm it from the ATAK app (Emergency / Alert tool). By contrast, on AINA PTT Voice Responders (V1 and V2), a 1-second long press on the speaker-mic disables / turns off an active emergency directly.
    • Top / group switch button is unmapped. Only the primary PTT and SOS buttons are mapped.
    • No secondary channel (VS2) button.

    Pairing (one-step Classic Bluetooth)¶

    Pair the BT02 in Android Bluetooth Settings just like an AINA speaker-mic — do not use XV's "Scan for BLE PTT device" button:

    1. Power the Inrico BT02 completely off.
    2. Press and hold the Power button (~5–6 seconds) until the LED flashes red and blue alternating (Classic pairing mode).
    3. Open Android Settings → Connected devices → Pair new device and pair the B02PTT-xxxx Classic Bluetooth audio entry (with Headset / Call audio).
    4. Note: The BT02 also advertises an unbonded BLE peripheral with the same B02PTT-xxxx name. If Android hangs while pairing, make sure the LED is flashing red/blue (Classic pairing mode) and you are pairing the Classic audio entry rather than the BLE-only advertisement.
    5. Open ATAK / XVoice. B02PTT-xxxx appears in the Primary Speaker-Mic picker just like an AINA. When selected (or auto-connected), XVoice routes HFP audio to the Classic device and automatically discovers and connects to the companion BLE button link in the background.

    Button mapping¶

    Physical button Action Notes
    PTT (side) Transmit on primary channel Press and hold to talk; release to stop.
    SOS / Emergency Fire ATAK emergency beacon Press and hold 2 seconds to fire. Fire-only — cancel active emergency in ATAK's Emergency tool.
    Top / group button Unmapped Intentionally ignored.
    Volume +/- Local speaker volume Handled on the speaker-mic hardware.
